    How it works

    1. Your words or photo become a vector.A CLIP model turns text and images into 512 numbers in the same space, so "red sneaker" and a photo of one land close together.
    2. The model was fine-tuned on Amazon listings.Trained with a contrastive loss on 109,395 product photo and title pairs from the Amazon Berkeley Objects dataset, so it learned how products are actually described.
    3. A compressed index finds the nearest products.Every catalog photo is pre-embedded into a FAISS IVF-PQ index. A query is compared against all 121,549 products.
    4. It all runs in your browser.The encoders are exported to ONNX and quantized to INT8, then run with ONNX Runtime Web. The index ships as about 7 MB of arrays and is scanned in JavaScript. The same model is also packaged as a Dockerised FastAPI service, which I deployed and load-tested on AWS EC2.
